## Brand & Style

This design system drives a defiant, high-voltage career platform built for engineers and creators tired of sterile corporate templates. The aesthetic rejects muted enterprise beige in favor of a cyber-editorial, neo-brutalist ethos that is deliberately unapologetic, electric, and tactically structured. It blends technical nostalgia (honoring the raw power of legacy backend architectures like Servlets, Java, and XML schemas) with contemporary hyper-kinetic digital typography.

The target audience includes assertive developers, unconventional creatives, and ambitious operators who view their career narrative as a high-impact launch sequence rather than an obituary-style CV. Key characteristics include:
- **Neo-Brutalist Structural Rigor:** Chunky 2px–3px stark black boundaries, deliberate hard drop-shadow offsets, and visible modular grids.
- **Cyber-Editorial Typographic Drama:** Aggressive, wide grotesque display headlines paired with laser-precise monospace syntax labels.
- **Tactile Hyper-Responsiveness:** Components behave like physical mechanical switches—snapping on click, displacing down along shadow axes, and utilizing high-contrast inverted states.
- **Electric Micro-Accents:** Voltage highlights in electric yellow, acid cyan, and violet signal real-time ATS optimization, skill validation, and interactive prompts.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around extreme contrast and high-saturation energy. Light mode serves as the primary canvas to emulate high-grade technical newsprint and zine publication, accented with electric digital signals.

- **Primary (`#0022FF` Electric Cobalt):** Used for focal action drivers, hyperlinked elements, structural card highlights, and dominant brand banners.
- **Secondary (`#CCFF00` High-Voltage Neon Yellow):** Acts as the critical attention trigger—used for sticky badges, ATS score tags, active tooltips, and highlighted text runs.
- **Tertiary (`#7928CA` Deep Cyber Violet / `#00F0FF` Electric Cyan):** Micro-accents reserved for code parsing attributes, technical badge categories (e.g., `<schema>`, `JSP`, `RUNTIME`), and gradient edge glows.
- **Neutral (`#08080A` Ink Black):** The structural anchor. Applied as solid 2px–3px outlines, hard box shadows, and deep carbon backgrounds for terminal-style code previews.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF` & `#F4F4F9`):** Pure clinical white for interactive components and high-readability zones, set against off-white tinted paper backgrounds to prevent optical glare under pure neon accents.

Never dilute colors with soft pastel blends; color boundaries must remain stark and immediate.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the raw expressive geometry of **Syne** for headlines, the structured clarity of **Space Grotesk** for readable narrative copy, and the precise mechanical cadence of **JetBrains Mono** for structural data, metadata, and code accents.

- **Syne (Headlines):** Used in uppercase or heavy weights for dramatic impact. Pair critical keywords with deliberate italics to introduce swagger and editorial rhythm.
- **Space Grotesk (Body):** Balances geometric quirks with high legibility across long-form bullet points, job descriptions, and user profile bios.
- **JetBrains Mono (Metadata, Tags, Metrics):** Renders all dates, ATS percentages, technical tags (`<Servlet/>`, `web.xml`, `DAO`), and input labels. All label tokens default to monospaced text to reinforce engineering precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an assertive 12-column responsive grid on desktop and a high-density 4-column grid on mobile. 

- **Grid Discipline:** Columns and cards align along high-visibility horizontal and vertical axes. Heavy container outlines often share borders or snap together like modular rack units.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Layout blocks occasionally break symmetric flow by using `space-lg` or `space-xl` margins on alternating sides to produce a zine-inspired rhythm.
- **Reflow Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** Full 12 columns with split panes (interactive resume builder on the left, live reactive print preview on the right). Canvas margin is `2.5rem`.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, collapsing sidebar navigation into a fixed, heavy-bordered dock at the viewport edge.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with edge-to-edge modular cards. Gutters drop to `1rem`, and canvas margins scale down to `1.25rem`. Content stacks vertically, prioritizing rapid section editing through tabbed pill toggles.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects soft, diffused ambient drop shadows in favor of **tactile hard-offset neo-brutalist projection**. Depth is physical, mechanical, and binary.

- **Hard-Angle Offset:** Elevate containers using solid `#08080A` drop shadows without blur (e.g., `4px 4px 0px 0px #08080A` for base cards, `6px 6px 0px 0px #08080A` for prominent modals and floating toolbars).
- **Physical Depression on Action:** Active and pressed states compress the shadow down to `0px 0px 0px 0px #08080A` while translating the component along both the X and Y axes by `+4px`, simulating the mechanical snap of a tactile keyboard switch.
- **Electric Underglows:** Modals and critical floating alerts occasionally layer a secondary hard neon offset beneath the black shadow (e.g., `8px 8px 0px 0px #CCFF00`) to create high-visibility layered separation.
- **Layer Stacking (Z-Index Hierarchy):**
  1. Base Document Canvas: Matte background with subtle grid guide lines.
  2. Modular Surface Cards: Solid white or slate surface with 2.5px solid black border.
  3. Interactive Controls / Floating Shelves: Hard-shadowed panels resting above the page flow.
  4. Diagnostics & Live ATS Overlays: Top-tier draw layers with stark black outlines and vivid yellow corner tags.

## Shapes

The design system merges sharp structural architecture with hyper-tactile, rounded pill geometries. This contrast prevents the interface from feeling stiffly utilitarian while retaining its industrial punch.

- **Containers & Cards:** Utilize `rounded-md` (0.5rem) corners bounded by uniform 2px to 3px solid ink-black strokes (`#08080A`).
- **Interactive Badges & Skill Pills:** Employ full pill contours (`rounded-full` / 9999px) paired with hard black borders, creating physical, collectible "token" aesthetics for technical skill tags (e.g., `Java EE`, `Spring Boot`, `XML Schema`).
- **Input Fields & Form Elements:** Kept at a precise 0.5rem radius to frame data cleanly without looking soft or juvenile.
- **Cut & Angled Embellishments:** Critical status tags and metric badges may use 45-degree chamfered corners or industrial tab tabs along their upper boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Buttons:** Electric cobalt blue (`#0022FF`) background, crisp white typography (`Syne` or `JetBrains Mono` uppercase bold), 2.5px solid `#08080A` border, and a `4px 4px 0px #08080A` hard offset shadow. On `:hover`, translate -2px, -2px with an expanded 6px shadow. On `:active`, snap to `0px 0px` offset with zero translate.
- **Secondary (High-Voltage) Buttons:** High-voltage neon yellow (`#CCFF00`) fill with `#08080A` bold text. Same mechanical snap feedback.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Buttons:** Transparent background, 2px solid `#08080A` border, filling with `#CCFF00` or `#0022FF` on hover.

### Chips & Skill Tokens
- Pill-shaped (`9999px`), 1.5px solid `#08080A` outline, containing monospaced text.
- Tech tags display an opening and closing syntax format (e.g., `<Java17/>`, `[Servlet]`). Active or selected chips switch to full `#CCFF00` fill with a `2px 2px 0px #08080A` micro-shadow.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Thick white background, 2px black border, padded with `space-sm` vertically and `space-md` horizontally. Monospaced labels float above input frames in all-caps.
- **Focus State:** Border expands to `#0022FF` with a distinct `3px 3px 0px #08080A` offset drop and high-contrast label color inversion.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp or minimally rounded boxes (2px radius) with 2px solid `#08080A` borders. Checked state triggers an immediate solid `#CCFF00` fill with a bold black checkmark or inset square.

### Cards & Resume Section Blocks
- Bounded by 2.5px black borders with `4px 4px 0px #08080A` shadow. Card headers feature solid contrasting banners (e.g., cobalt header bar with white text) separated by a horizontal 2px line from the white content card body.

### ATS Score Metric Badges
- High-visibility status monitors styled like retro-futuristic voltage gauges. Rendered with black backgrounds, glowing neon text, monospace percentages, and neon-yellow pill tags indicating parsed optimization levels.
